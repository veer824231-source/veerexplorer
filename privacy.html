Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how Veer Explorer collects, uses, protects, and manages information when you interact with our platform, services, applications, and digital experiences.

We may collect basic usage information, browser details, device characteristics, performance metrics, and interaction data required to improve service reliability, user experience, and security monitoring. Personal information is only collected when voluntarily submitted by users.

2

Information is used to operate services, improve performance, enhance security, provide customer support, optimize user experiences, and maintain system stability. Data may also be analyzed in aggregate form for research and service improvements.

3

Data Security

We implement modern security measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, modification, or destruction. While no online system is completely immune from risk, we continuously monitor and improve our security infrastructure.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to remember preferences, improve functionality, analyze traffic patterns, and provide a smoother browsing experience. Users may manage cookie settings through their browser preferences.

5

6

User Rights

Depending on applicable laws, users may have rights to access, update, correct, or request deletion of their personal data. Requests can be submitted through official communication channels.

7

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational, legal, or technological changes. Continued use of our services following updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

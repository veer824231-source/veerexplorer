Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Veer Explorer uses cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, personalize user experiences, enhance security, and analyze platform performance.

1

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember preferences, improve performance, and provide a more personalized browsing experience.

2

How We Use Cookies

Veer Explorer uses cookies to maintain essential site functionality, remember user settings, improve security, measure traffic, and enhance overall platform performance.

Essential Cookies

Required for basic website functionality and security.

Preference Cookies

Save user settings and customization preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Help understand traffic patterns and user behavior.

Performance Cookies

Improve speed, reliability, and platform stability.

3

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services integrated into our platform may use their own cookies for analytics, embedded content, security, or functionality purposes. These cookies are governed by the respective providers' policies.

4

Managing Cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, block, delete, or control cookies through browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality and performance of the platform.

5

Data Protection

Cookies used by Veer Explorer do not intentionally collect sensitive personal information. Any data gathered through cookies is handled according to our Privacy Policy and applicable data protection requirements.

6

Policy Updates

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, regulations, or platform features. Continued use of the website indicates acceptance of any future updates.
